I am an AI Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://www.cs.ucla.edu/), collaborating with [Bolei Zhou](https://boleizhou.github.io/) (UCLA VAIL), [Trevor Darrell](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~trevor/) (UC Berkeley BAIR) and [Jiaqi Ma](https://mobility-lab.seas.ucla.edu/about/) (UCLA Mobility-Lab). My work has been widely featured by media outlets such as VentureBeat, Vice, MarkTechPost, and Weights & Biases.   <p>My research lies at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, and computer graphics. I aim to develop <strong style="color: rgb(255, 60, 0);">general physical AI systems</strong> that are <strong style="color: rgb(255, 60, 0);">scalable, agentic, and safe</strong> in the open world. In the long term, I envision intelligent mobility and service systems that assist people in everyday urban life and make cities more efficient, sustainable, and accessible.</p>

  <p>To realize this vision, I focus on three fundamental challenges: scaling the world models and environments in which agents learn, enabling agents to learn and adapt through embodied experience, and ensuring safe interaction with people.</p>

  <ul>
    <li><strong>Scalable World Models &amp; Simulators</strong>: Building large-scale, interactive robot-learning platforms from real-world videos and diverse digital assets, with open-ended urban environments for efficient training and evaluation, as in <a href="https://urbanverseproject.github.io/">UrbanVerse</a>, <a href="https://metadriverse.github.io/urban-sim/">URBAN-SIM</a>, <a href="https://metadriverse.github.io/metaurban/">MetaUrban</a>, <a href="https://metadriverse.github.io/vid2sim/">Vid2Sim</a>, and <a href="https://omniobject3d.github.io/">OmniObject3D</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Agentic Physical Intelligence</strong>: Building adaptive embodied agents that perceive and reason about both their internal states and external environments, plan and act through physical skills, and self-improve through interaction and experience, as in <a href="https://alvinyh.github.io/VGHuman/">Visually Grounded Humanoid Agents</a> and <a href="https://embodiedhuman.github.io/">EmbodiedHuman</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Human-Centered Safety</strong>: Modeling people across appearance, motion, and interactive behavior, from high-fidelity 4D humans to reactive pedestrians; and developing human-aware robots that operate safely and reliably in populated environments, as in <a href="https://dna-rendering.github.io/">DNA-Rendering</a>, <a href="https://synbody.github.io/">SynBody</a>, <a href="https://genforce.github.io/PedGen/">PedGen</a>, and <a href="https://vail-ucla.github.io/SidewalkBench/">SidewalkBench</a>.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
